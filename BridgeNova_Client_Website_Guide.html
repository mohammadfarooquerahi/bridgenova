Section 1: Access & Architecture

1 Introduction & Core Architecture

Your BridgeNova website has been engineered specifically for effortless client management. You do not need any coding or web development experience to keep your site updated, replace photos, add events, change FAQ questions, or publish new announcements.

Dual-Layer Content Management System Your website provides two intuitive management methods tailored to different types of content:
• Theme Customizer ("Theme Options"): Real-time visual editor for the Homepage, Hero banner, Impact counters, FAQ accordion, Contact info, and Footer.
• WordPress Page Editor (`the_content()`): Standard visual block editor for custom page content (About Us, Services, Contact, Team, Partners, Speakers, Events).

How to Access the WordPress Admin Dashboard

1

Navigate to Admin URL

Open your browser and navigate to https://yourdomain.com/wp-admin (or /wp-login.php).

2

Sign In with Client Credentials

Enter your Administrator username and password. You will arrive at the main WordPress Dashboard.

3

Access Direct Edit Links

In the left sidebar, look for Appearance > Theme Options (Edit All Sections). Alternatively, when viewing your live website while logged in, simply click Customize in the top black admin bar.

Section 2: Visual Theme Options

3 Editing via Visual Theme Options

The BridgeNova Theme Options panel is built into the native WordPress Customizer. As you type changes or upload new photos, a live preview on the right shows exactly how the website will look before you publish.

How to Open the Visual Customizer: Go to Appearance > Theme Options (Edit All Sections), or open your homepage and click Customize at the top of your screen.
01 Hero Section

Eyebrow Text: The gold uppercase subtitle (e.g. Global Connections).
Hero Title: Main heading (e.g. Building Bridges Across Borders).
Description: Intro paragraph explaining BRIDGE's mission.
Hero Background Image: Click "Select Image" or "Upload" to change the high-resolution hero background photo.
Action Buttons 1, 2, 3: Configure button text and link URLs (e.g. Join BRIDGE, Event Registration, Contact Us).

02 Stats & Impact Counters

Configure the 4 large animated gold numbers displayed below the hero:
• Metric 1: Number (e.g. 59 or 500+) and Label (Members).
• Metric 2: Number (e.g. 11 or 30+) and Label (Countries).
• Metric 3: Number (e.g. 10 or 50+) and Label (Programs).
• Metric 4: Number (e.g. 20 or 100+) and Label (Partners).

03 About BRIDGE Section

Controls the introductory showcase on the homepage:
• Section Eyebrow: (e.g. About BRIDGE)
• Section Main Title: (e.g. Building Bridges for a Better Future)
• Paragraph 1: Primary mission and organizational statement.
• Paragraph 2: Secondary impact and reach statement.

04 Six Strategic Pillars

Allows updating titles and detailed descriptions for all six framework cards:
• Pillar 01: Leadership Development
• Pillar 02: International Exchange
• Pillar 03: Global Dialogue & Diplomacy
• Pillar 04: Innovation & Entrepreneurship
• Pillar 05: Global Careers
• Pillar 06: Community Development & Impact

Publishing Your Customizer Edits

Instant Live Updating: When you are satisfied with your edits in the preview, click the blue "Publish" button at the top of the customizer sidebar. Your changes immediately go live to all visitors.

Section 3: FAQ, Social & Contact Info

4 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Accordion

The FAQ section is fully dynamic. The accordion automatically features smooth animated expand/collapse functionality with gold rotation indicators. You can manage questions 1 through 8 directly from the Customizer.

1

Open FAQ Panel

Go to Appearance > Theme Options > Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).

2

Edit Questions and Answers

Each FAQ has a dedicated Question text field and Answer text box. You can modify any existing answer or rewrite questions at any time.

3

Hiding or Deleting an FAQ

If you have fewer than 8 FAQs, simply leave the Question field blank. The website will automatically hide that FAQ accordion item so no empty space appears.

5 Contact Details, Social Media & Footer

Contact & Location Information

Controls contact information shown across the Header, Contact Page, and Footer:
• Email Address: Central inquiry email (e.g. info@bridgeglobalnetwork.org).
• Phone Number: Official phone / WhatsApp line.
• Office Location: Physical city/country (e.g. Islamabad, Pakistan).

Social Media Channels

Enter the direct URLs to your organization's social profiles. Leaving any URL blank will hide that icon automatically:
• Facebook: https://facebook.com/yourpage
• Instagram: https://instagram.com/yourhandle
• LinkedIn: https://linkedin.com/company/...
• YouTube & X (Twitter): Full channel URLs.

Footer Brand Overview & Copyright Notice

Located under Appearance > Theme Options > Footer & Copyright:
• Footer Bio / Description: A short paragraph describing BRIDGE at the bottom left of the footer.
• Copyright Line: The copyright statement (e.g. © 2026 Bridge Global Network. All rights reserved.). The theme automatically keeps the current year up to date.

Section 4: Editing Individual Pages

6 Editing Pages via WordPress Page Editor

All page templates have been built with full native support for WordPress's page content loop (the_content()). You can easily add text, images, video embeds, download buttons, or custom notices to any page.

Step-by-Step: Updating a Page

1

Locate the Page in WordPress Admin

From your WordPress dashboard, navigate to Pages > All Pages. Hover over the page you want to update (e.g. About Us, Services, Our Team, or Contact Us) and click Edit.

2

Add or Edit Content

Use the standard WordPress Block Editor (Gutenberg) to type paragraphs, insert bulleted lists, add image blocks, embed YouTube videos, or paste notices.

3

Update and Save

Click the blue "Update" button in the top right corner. Your added content will seamlessly display on that page styled to match the dark navy and gold theme aesthetic.

7 Managing Dynamic Content: Events & Team

Managing Upcoming Events

Go to Events > Add New in the left sidebar:
• Title: Event Name (e.g. BRIDGE Youth Leadership Summit 2026).
• Featured Image: Upload a high-quality landscape image (16:9 aspect ratio recommended).
• Excerpt / Meta: Add the event date, time, and location (e.g. October 24, 2026 • Islamabad).
Published events automatically appear in the 3-column event grid on the homepage and on the /events-bridge/ page.

Managing Team Members

Go to Team Members > Add New:
• Title: Member's full name.
• Featured Image: Headshot photo (portrait aspect ratio recommended).
• Content Body: Member biography and qualifications.
• Excerpt / Custom Fields: Position or title (e.g. Executive Director) and LinkedIn profile URL.

8 Navigation & Dropdown Menu Controls

The primary menu features a single dropdown indicator (▼) with smooth 180° hover rotation on desktop and responsive touch accordion support on mobile devices.

To Rearrange or Add Menu Items: Go to Appearance > Menus. Select the Primary Menu. Drag items up, down, or indent them to the right to create dropdown submenus. Click Save Menu.

I edited a section in the Customizer, but don't see it on my phone?

Ensure you clicked the blue "Publish" button in the Customizer. Then, perform a hard refresh on your mobile browser by clearing cache or opening in a private/incognito window.

?

How do I hide an FAQ that is no longer relevant?

Go to Appearance > Theme Options > Frequently Asked Questions and delete the text inside the Question field for that item. The website will automatically hide that accordion row.

?

Where do Contact Us and Event Registration submissions go?

Form submissions are delivered immediately to the administrator email address configured in Settings > General and in the Theme Options Contact Email field.
